# Vibe & Logistics Consolidation Spec (DF - Dynamic Form)

## Objective
Consolidate the current Step 2 (Travel Persona & Energy) and Step 3 (Budget & Noctourism) into a single, cohesive "Trip Customization" screen.

## Layout Concept
- **Container**: A high-contrast, dual-column grid (on desktop) or stacked sections (on mobile).
- **Left Column: The Vibe**
  - **Travel Persona Selection**: Visual cards for solo, couple, foodie, nomad.
  - **Energy Level Slider**: Interactive slider with text feedback (Chill to Active).
- **Right Column: The Setup**
  - **Budget Selection**: 3-tier selection ($, $$, $$$).
  - **Noctourism Toggle**: Feature-style toggle for evening priorities.

## Benefits
- Reduces onboarding steps from 3 to 2.
- Provides a comprehensive overview of the "Travel Identity" in one go.
- Smoother transition from "Where to" to "How to".

## Component Name
`TripVibeForm` or `VibeSelector`.
